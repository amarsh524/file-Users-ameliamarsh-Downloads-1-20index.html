CORE 3500: Wealth Building Glossary & Account Cheat Sheet

1. 401(k) / 403(b) + Employer Match

"The Free Money Hack"
  • The Vibe: Pre-tax money taken straight out of your paycheck before you even see it (so you can’t accidentally spend it at Target).
  • The Match: Your employer matches what you save up to a cap. If you put in 5% and they put in 10%, that’s literally 100% free money (a 2:1 match). Skipping the match is like turning down a free paycheck. Don't do it!

2. Roth IRA

"Tax-Free Future Bags"
  • The Vibe: You put in money you’ve already paid taxes on today. It sits in your account, grows over time, and when you pull it out in retirement, you pay $0 in taxes on all those gains.
  • The Flex: You can withdraw what you put in (your principal) anytime tax-free and penalty-free if an absolute emergency pops up.

3. Taxable Brokerage Account

"The Main Character Account"
  • The Vibe: Total freedom with zero rules. No annual contribution limits, no income caps, and no waiting until you're 59½ to touch your money.
  • The Trade-off: There are no special tax perks here. You pay taxes on profits when you sell, but it's perfect for big medium-term goals like buying a home, starting a business, or retiring early.

4. HSA (Health Savings Account)

"The Ultimate Triple-Threat Cheat Code"
  • The Vibe: An account for High-Deductible Health Plans that gives you a triple tax advantage: pre-tax contributions, tax-free growth, and tax-free spending on healthcare (therapy, glasses, prescriptions, sunscreen, etc.).
  • The Flex: Unused money NEVER expires. It rolls over every year and can be invested in the market like a stealth retirement fund.

5. FSA (Flexible Spending Account)

"The Use-It-or-Lose-It Wallet"
  • The Vibe: Pre-tax payroll money set aside for health costs. It cuts down your taxable income right now.
  • The Trap: Strict deadlines! Unused funds disappear at year-end if you don't spend them (use-it-or-lose-it rule). Only contribute what you know you'll use.

6. FICA Tax (Social Security & Medicare)

"The Non-Negotiable Community Subscription"
  • The Vibe: A standard 7.65% flat deduction taken straight out of every paycheck.
  • Where It Goes: 6.2% funds Social Security (senior/disability benefits) and 1.45% funds Medicare (senior healthcare). You can't change or opt out of this tax—it applies to all traditional employment.
